这是什么游戏？

烬灯行是一款 iPhone 和 iPad 上的横版像素动作游戏。你要打过七章，从七位守灯人手里夺回七盏灯。

怎么操作？

左手摇杆移动，右手六个按钮：斩、跃、闪、挡、技、壶。敌人兵刃上闪白光，就迎上去按“挡”把它弹开；闪红光，就按“闪”躲开。游戏里按暂停，在“操作”页可以随时查看全部说明。也支持键盘和手柄。

进度存在哪里？

存档只保存在这台设备上，不会上传，也不会在设备之间同步。删除应用会一起删除存档。

可以换语言吗？

可以。在标题页或暂停菜单的“设置”里，可以在中文和英文之间切换。

需要什么设备？要联网吗？

需要 iOS 16 或 iPadOS 16 及以上的 iPhone 或 iPad，横屏游玩。游戏不需要联网。

要花钱吗？

游戏免费，没有广告，没有内购。

What is Emberlight?

Emberlight is a 2D pixel action game for iPhone and iPad. You fight through seven chapters to take seven lamps back from their keepers.

How do I play?

Move with the stick on the left. On the right are six buttons: strike, jump, roll, guard, art and heal. When an enemy's weapon glints white, meet it with guard to parry; when it flares red, roll away. Pause the game and open Controls to see everything at any time. Keyboards and game controllers also work.

Where is my progress saved?

Your save stays on this device only. It is not uploaded and does not sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Settings on the title screen or in the pause menu to switch between Chinese and English.

What do I need? Does it need the internet?

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 16 or iPadOS 16 or later, held in landscape. The game does not need a network connection.

Does it cost anything?

The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, system version and game version.
